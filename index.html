Why they all stayed

Spay. Neuter. Stay.

The vet gave Brad the numbers. What happens to black cats. How many end up in shelters. How many don't come out.

Brad didn't need his arm twisted.

Here's what you have to understand about us. Between the two of us we have raised eight children. We were done. The house was supposed to be quiet now.

In June of 2025 it was quiet. Just not the way anyone wants.

So we made two decisions. The first one was responsible — Nova spayed, Diva spayed, the boys neutered. No more. Not one more.

The second one wasn't about being responsible. It was about being fair.

Nova was six months old. She should have been somebody's kitten. Instead she was a mother — starving, hunted, holding four lives together on a body that had nothing left in it. Nobody asked her if she wanted any of it.

So we asked the only question we still had the power to answer.

Does she get to keep them?

Yes. She keeps them. All five stay.

The story that started everything

Some moments aren't random.
They're assignments.

A Ring camera caught a small black cat on the deck — terrified, feral, barely six months old — trying to keep four babies alive on a body that had nothing left to give. At 2 AM she brought all four to the garage. We closed the door. Five terrified lives came in that night.

Tex

2019 – 8 April 2026 · The Cross-Eyed Fugitive From Texas

He got under a truck in Texas and rode it to Georgia — uninvited, undeterred, and absolutely furious about the situation. Nobody sent for him. Nobody asked. He made a decision and it led here: to a plaid collar, a backyard, and people who loved him so hard it still hurts.

Good boy. Chaotic boy. Our boy.
